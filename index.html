About Our Field Trip

Located in Ontario's agricultural region, This Boreal Smart Farm combines traditional farming with brand-new cutting-edge technology. Our class explored greenhouses, learned about sensors, and saw how AI helps farmers make better decisions.

This website shares what we learned, from hydroponic systems to automated climate control.

  • Explored automated greenhouse systems
  • Learned about AI-powered crop monitoring
  • Saw hydroponic and vertical farming
  • Discovered sensor-based plant tracking
